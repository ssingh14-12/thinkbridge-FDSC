About thinkbridge

thinkbridge is a global digital product development firm that helps growth-stage companies gain the technology sophistication of leading modern digital businesses. The firm differentiates through exceptional quality at scale and speed with its ThinkStack accelerators.

The vision: build amazing software fast. A new way to build — focused on outcomes, not tools, methodologies, or vanity artifacts.

About the Role

The Forward Deployed Solutions Consultant embeds with mid-market customers in the $25M–$150M revenue range, understands how their business actually runs, and delivers working prototypes that close the gap between the software they bought and the operating layer they actually need.

You sit between the customer and the engineering team — translating business needs into shippable prototypes using AI-native tools (Claude, Cursor, v0, Lovable) — then hand off clean specifications to engineering for production build.

Key Responsibilities
01
Customer Integration
Embed with client teams to understand their workflows, infrastructure, and unique challenges — sitting in their meetings and observing the work as it happens.
02
Discovery & Process Design
Lead discovery sessions with stakeholders from frontline operators to C-suite, map current-state processes, and design future-state workflows in the customer's own vocabulary.
03
Technical Partnership
Serve as the primary contact bridging the gap between the client's business goals and product capabilities across the full engagement.
04
Prototype Delivery
Build functional prototypes using AI-assisted tools and no-code platforms such as Zapier, Airtable, and Make — on tight, one-week iteration loops.
05
Specification Authoring
Author technical specifications, BRDs, and FRDs that the engineering team can build from without ambiguity or back-and-forth.
06
Engineering Handoff
Partner with the engineering team during production build to ensure prototype intent translates cleanly to delivery.
07
Stakeholder Communication
Communicate trade-offs, timelines, and risks clearly to both technical and business stakeholders — from developer to CEO.
Qualifications
Required
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or related field — or equivalent experience
  • 3–5 years in business process analysis, product management, solutions consulting, or implementation consulting
  • Demonstrated experience shipping with AI tools (Claude API, OpenAI, Cursor, or equivalent)
  • Strong process design skills: BRDs, FRDs, UML diagrams, data mapping, current- and future-state workflow modeling
  • Experience facilitating stakeholder workshops and translating business requirements into technical specifications
  • Proven ability to communicate effectively with C-suite executives and engineering teams
  • Comfortable operating in ambiguous, fast-moving environments with evolving objectives
Preferred
  • Hands-on with no-code automation — Zapier, Airtable, Make, n8n
  • Familiarity with mid-market CRM and ERP — Salesforce, Workday, SAP, Zoho
  • Basic SQL or Python for data analysis and prototyping
  • Portfolio of AI-built artifacts or shipped projects you can walk through
