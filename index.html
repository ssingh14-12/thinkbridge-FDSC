About the Role

The Forward Deployed Solutions Consultant embeds with mid-market customers in the $25M–$150M revenue range, understands how their business actually runs, and delivers working prototypes that close the gap between the software they bought and the operating layer they need.

In this role, you apply problem-solving ability, creativity, and technical skills to help organizations move from operational friction to working solutions. You sit between the customer and the engineering team, translating business needs into shippable prototypes using AI-native tools — vibe coding with Claude, Cursor, v0, and Lovable — then hand off clean specifications to engineering for production build.

Key Responsibilities
  • Customer Integration — Embed with client teams to understand their workflows, infrastructure, and unique challenges, sitting in their meetings and observing the work as it happens.
  • Discovery & Process Design — Lead discovery sessions with stakeholders from frontline operators to C-suite, map current-state processes, and design future-state workflows in the customer's own vocabulary.
  • Technical Partnership — Serve as the primary contact bridging the gap between the client's business goals and product capabilities across the engagement.
  • Prototype Delivery — Build functional prototypes using AI-assisted tools and no-code platforms such as Zapier, Airtable, and Make, on tight iteration loops.
  • Specification Authoring — Author technical specifications, BRDs, and FRDs that the engineering team can build from without ambiguity.
  • Engineering Handoff — Partner with the engineering team during production build to ensure prototype intent translates to delivery.
  • Stakeholder Communication — Communicate trade-offs, timelines, and risks to both technical and business stakeholders.
Required Qualifications
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or related field — or equivalent relevant experience
  • 3–5 years of experience in business process analysis, product management, solutions consulting, or implementation consulting
  • Demonstrated experience building or shipping with AI tools — Claude API, OpenAI, Cursor, or equivalent
  • Strong process design skills: BRDs, FRDs, UML diagrams, data mapping, current-state and future-state workflow modeling
  • Experience facilitating stakeholder workshops and translating business requirements into technical specifications
  • Proven ability to communicate effectively with C-suite executives and engineering teams
  • Comfortable operating in ambiguous, fast-moving environments with evolving objectives
Preferred Qualifications
  • Hands-on experience with no-code automation tools — Zapier, Airtable, Make, n8n
  • Familiarity with mid-market CRM and ERP platforms — Salesforce, Workday, SAP, Zoho
  • Basic SQL or Python for data analysis and prototyping
  • Portfolio of AI-built artifacts or shipped projects you can walk through
What You Will Own

  • Quality and clarity of every technical specification delivered to engineering
  • Customer satisfaction and adoption outcomes for assigned engagements
