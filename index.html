About the Role
We are hiring a Forward Deployed Solutions Engineer to work directly with mid-market customers, understand their workflows, and deliver working prototypes that solve real operational problems. You will sit between the customer and our engineering team, translating business needs into shippable solutions using AI-native tools.

This is a customer-facing, hands-on role for someone who can move from discovery to working prototype quickly, then hand off to engineering for production build. No one at thinkbridge has held this title before — you will define what it means.
Key Responsibilities
  • 🔍
    Deep Customer Discovery — Conduct discovery sessions with stakeholders from frontline operators to C-suite, mapping pain points, workflows, and business priorities in 1-week sprint cycles.
  • 🗺️
    Process Design — Map current-state processes and design future-state workflows using process diagrams, data flow models, and user journey maps.
  • ⚡
    AI-Native Prototyping — Build functional prototypes using Claude, Cursor, v0, and Lovable — compressing the discovery-to-prototype cycle from months to days.
  • 📄
    Technical Specification Authoring — Write BRDs, FRDs, and OpenAPI specs that engineering teams can build from without ambiguity or back-and-forth.
  • 🤝
    Engineering Handoff & Partnership — Collaborate with engineering during production build to ensure prototype intent translates cleanly into delivery.
  • 📊
    Stakeholder Communication — Communicate trade-offs, timelines, and risks clearly to both technical and business audiences — from developer to CEO.
Required Qualifications
3-5 years combined experience in business process analysis, product management, solutions consulting, or implementation consulting
Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or related field (Master's preferred)
Demonstrated AI tool experience — Claude API, OpenAI, Cursor, or equivalent; portfolio of AI-built artifacts preferred
Strong process design skills: BRDs, FRDs, UML diagrams, data mapping, current- and future-state workflow modeling
Experience facilitating stakeholder workshops and translating business requirements into technical specifications
Comfortable operating in ambiguous, fast-moving environments with dynamic objectives and weekly iteration with users
Preferred Qualifications
Hands-on experience with no-code automation tools: Zapier, Airtable, Make, n8n
Familiarity with mid-market CRM/ERP: Salesforce, Workday, SAP, Zoho
Basic SQL or Python for data analysis and prototyping

Why thinkbridge
thinkbridge has spent over a decade building custom operating layers for mid-market companies. With AI tools now mature enough to compress the discovery-to-prototype cycle from months to days, we are expanding our delivery model to include forward-deployed solutions work.

This role is central to that expansion. You will have direct access to founders, full AI tooling access, and the opportunity to define a new function from scratch — with the backing of an engineering team and a decade of customer relationships behind you.
