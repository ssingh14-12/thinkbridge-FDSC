About the Role

We are hiring a Forward Deployed Solutions Engineer (FDSE) to embed with mid-market customers in the $25M–$150M revenue range, understand how their business actually runs, and close the gap between the software they bought and the operating layer they need.

As an FDSE, you will apply your problem-solving ability, creativity, and technical skills to translate business needs into shippable prototypes using AI-native tools (Claude, Cursor, etc.), then hand off clean specifications to engineering for production build. This is a customer-facing, hands-on role for builders who ship fast and partner deeply with engineering.

Key Responsibilities
01
Discovery Sessions
Conduct discovery sessions with customer stakeholders, from frontline operators to C-suite executives, to understand workflows, pain points, and business priorities.
02
Process & Workflow Design
Map current-state processes and design future-state workflows using process diagrams, data flow models, and user journey maps.
03
Prototype Delivery
Build functional prototypes using AI-assisted development tools (Claude, Cursor, etc.) on fast iteration loops.
04
Specification Authoring
Author technical specifications, BRDs, and FRDs that engineering teams can build from without ambiguity.
05
Engineering Partnership
Partner with the engineering team during production build to ensure prototype intent translates cleanly to delivery.
06
Cross-functional Collaboration
Collaborate across technical and non-technical teams in a constantly evolving environment with dynamic objectives and continuous iteration with users.
07
Stakeholder Communication
Communicate trade-offs, timelines, and risks clearly to both technical and business stakeholders.
